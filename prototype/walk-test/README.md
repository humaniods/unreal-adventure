# Triund walk test (browser prototype)

The real Triund trek in the browser (three.js r128): McLeod Ganj Main Square → Gallu Devi → Triund top → Snowline Cafe,
or up from Bhagsu. Terrain, trails, roads, buildings and places come from OpenStreetMap and SRTM elevation
(`../geo/build.py`).

Run it: `python3 -m http.server 8765` in this folder, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/index.html (Solo only).
For friends, voice and talking to bots, run `../server/dev.sh` and open http://127.0.0.1:8787/ instead.

```
index.html            the page: start screen, HUD, terrain, player, camera, main loop
data/                 generated by ../geo/build.py
  triund-data.js        heights, trails, roads, marks, buildings, POIs
  sat-near.jpg          satellite colour of the play box
  sat-far.jpg           satellite colour of the surroundings
assets/
  chars/                people and animals (glTF, from ../geo/build_chars.py and build_animals.py)
  tex/                  ground and wall photo textures (Poly Haven, CC0; see SOURCES.txt)
js/
  core/                 engine pieces
    quality.js            graphics presets
    post.js               post-processing and dynamic resolution
    sky.js atmos.js       sky, clouds, mist
    weather.js season.js  time of day, weather, late-November snow
    terrain-material.js   ground shader
    anim-util.js          poses and leg IK
    collide.js            solid buildings, stalls, trees
    sound.js              streams, waterfalls, temples, mules, music
  world/                what stands in the world
    flora.js              trees, bushes, grass, stones
    steps.js              stone and concrete steps on the trails
    places.js             OSM buildings, stalls, temples, waterfalls
    town.js               McLeod Ganj and Dharamkot shop fronts, balconies, flags, poles, scooters
    signs.js trailprops.js  trail boards, selfie points, fences, tarps, boulders
    night.js              stars, town lights, camps and fires
    vehicles.js           cars, the road surface, cars driving a road
    fauna.js              dogs, mules, langurs, goats, birds
  people/
    crowd.js              hikers and shopkeepers
    hands.js              first-person hands (eating, drinking)
  net/                  playing together (needs ../server running; Solo works without it)
    net.js                Couple / Bakchods rooms by code, other players, name tags, friends, mic modes,
                          hold hands (J), carry your partner on your shoulders (U)
    voice.js              LiveKit voice: All (near you + team), Team, Off
    talk.js               Enter to chat with hikers and stall keepers (server /bot/chat, Claude; short lines offline)
  trip/                 gameplay
    economy.js            menu, prices, coins wallet, bag
    payments.js           coin top-up (mock gateway, test mode)
    trek.js               food energy, stalls, bag, game clock
    camp.js               tents and the night at the top
    trip.js               start options, Gallu check post, taxis and the ride, legs after the top
```
