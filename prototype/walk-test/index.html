Triund Walk Test

The real Triund trails above McLeod Ganj, up to the meadow at 2,838 m facing the Dhauladhar range. Walk up from McLeod Ganj Main Square through Dharamkot (or take a taxi), sign in at the forest check post at Gallu Devi and climb the classic path through oak and rhododendron forest, past Magic View Cafe and up the 22 curves; or take the steeper, quieter climb from Bhagsu past the waterfall, Shiva Cafe and Karthani. From the top, go on to Snowline Cafe, or walk down the other path to close the loop. There is no health bar. Your breathing, your heartbeat and the smartwatch on your wrist tell you how your body is holding up.

Written in the forest register at the Gallu check post. Entry ₹100, taxis, stalls and the camp are paid in coins from your phone (1 coin = ₹1).

Trails: OpenStreetMap route "Galu Devi – Triund", the OpenStreetMap paths from Bhagsu via Shiva Cafe and Karthani, the lanes from McLeod Ganj Main Square through Dharamkot and the "Triund Hill – SnowLine Cafe Trail" (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Entry fee and check post as reported for the Dharamshala Forest Circle (₹100 per person per day at Gallu); taxi fares are estimates. Temples, cafes, camps, streams and building outlines also come from OpenStreetMap; buildings OSM has not traced come from Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL); building heights and colours are estimates. Terrain: SRTM elevation via AWS Terrain Tiles at about 30 m detail, so the shape of the mountain is real but small steps and rocks are not. Imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. Ground textures: Poly Haven (CC0). People and animals: Quaternius (CC0) characters, animations and animal pack; clothes, crowd and behaviour are generated in the game. Clouds and sounds are generated in the game; their placement and behaviour follow trekkers' trip reports (October–November morning).
