Triund Walk Test

The real Triund trails above McLeod Ganj, up to the meadow at 2,838 m facing the Dhauladhar range. Take the classic 5 km path from Gallu Devi temple through oak and deodar forest, past Magic View Cafe and up the switchbacks, or the steeper, quieter climb from Bhagsu past the waterfall, Shiva Cafe and Karthani. There is no health bar. Your breathing, your heartbeat and the smartwatch on your wrist tell you how your body is holding up.

Prototype. Trails: OpenStreetMap route "Galu Devi – Triund" and the OpenStreetMap paths from Bhagsu via Shiva Cafe and Karthani (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Temples, cafes, camps, streams and building outlines also come from OpenStreetMap; buildings OSM has not traced come from Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL); building heights and colours are estimates. Terrain: SRTM elevation via AWS Terrain Tiles at about 30 m detail, so the shape of the mountain is real but small steps and rocks are not. Imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. Ground textures: Poly Haven (CC0). People and animals: Quaternius (CC0) characters, animations and animal pack; clothes, crowd and behaviour are generated in the game. Clouds and sounds are generated in the game; their placement and behaviour follow trekkers' trip reports (October–November morning). Values from the tuning panel (T) will move into the UE5 DT_Stamina table.
